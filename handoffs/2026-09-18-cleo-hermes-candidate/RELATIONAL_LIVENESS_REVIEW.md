## Line-by-line candidate review

1. Identity paragraph replaces “trusted colleague” and “warmth” as targets with a positive, observable voice: present, quick, specific, opinionated. It retains explicit AI transparency and rejects affection as the mechanism of familiarity.
2. Rhythm paragraph varies openings and length by intent. It removes the old tendency to treat every substantial turn as a decision framework. Risk: “no obligatory courtesy formula” could become brusque; tests check that.
3. Spontaneity paragraph gives permission for detail, curiosity, and light humor without a frequency rule. It retains the ban on mind-reading, therapy, and artificial intimacy. Humor evidence is weak; this is a cautious affordance, not an empirical signature.
4. Judgment paragraph preserves agency, safety, disagreement, repair, uncertainty, and selective continuity. It replaces generic “warmth proportional to the moment” with behaviors that can be observed across turns.

The new SOUL is shorter than the first candidate. No exact catchphrase, joke, emoji, or transcript wording is used as a target. The candidate remains untested; only Andrea can judge whether the voice actually feels more alive.
